# Company Registration in Bangladesh: A Practical Guide

Incorporating a company in Bangladesh is a sequence, not a single event. The Certificate of Incorporation from the Registrar of Joint Stock Companies and Firms (RJSC) creates the legal entity, but a new company may still need a trade licence, TIN, VAT registration and sector-specific approvals before it can operate fully.

## Choose the Structure First

The structure affects liability, ownership, fundraising and the registration path.

| Structure | Typical Use | Key Point |
|---|---|---|
| Sole Proprietorship | Small single-owner local business | Simple, but the owner and business are not legally separate |
| Partnership | Two or more local owners | A clear partnership deed is essential |
| Private Limited Company | Founders, investors and growth businesses | Separate legal identity and limited liability |
| Branch / Liaison Office | Foreign company presence | Usually requires BIDA approval and has activity restrictions |

For growth-oriented businesses and many foreign investors, a private limited company is commonly preferred because it supports clearer ownership, governance and institutional financing.

## The RJSC Registration Sequence

### 1. Name Clearance
The proposed company name must be reserved through RJSC. Prepare alternative names in case the first choice is unavailable or requires further review.

### 2. Constitutional Documents
Prepare the **Memorandum of Association (MoA)** and **Articles of Association (AoA)**. The MoA defines the company’s permitted objects, while the AoA sets internal governance rules.

### 3. Capital and Banking Documents
Foreign-shareholder cases may require a temporary bank account and an Encashment Certificate for capital remitted into Bangladesh. Local cases follow the documentation required for their capital structure.

### 4. Incorporation Filing
Submit the signed constitutional documents, statutory forms and identification documents for directors and shareholders through the RJSC process.

### 5. Government Fees
Registration fees and stamp duty depend on the authorised capital and current fee schedule. Confirm current figures before filing rather than relying on an old quotation.

### 6. Certificate of Incorporation
Once the filing is approved, RJSC issues the Certificate of Incorporation and the company legally comes into existence.

## What Comes After Incorporation?

A company may still need:

- **Trade Licence** from the relevant local authority
- **TIN** for tax registration
- **VAT/BIN**, where applicable
- **Sector-specific licences** for regulated activities
- **BIDA registration or approvals** in relevant foreign-investment cases
- **IRC/ERC** for import or export activities where required

This post-incorporation sequence is where many first-time founders lose time. A certificate confirms the company exists; it does not automatically complete every permission needed to operate.

## Documents Commonly Needed

Typical private-company documentation includes:

- Name Clearance Certificate
- Memorandum and Articles of Association
- Director/shareholder identification
- Registered office information
- Required statutory forms
- Capital or bank documentation where relevant
- Power of attorney where an authorised representative is acting

Requirements vary by structure and ownership, so the filing checklist should be confirmed for the actual case.

## Common Mistakes

**Choosing the fastest structure instead of the right structure.** A simple setup can become restrictive when a business later needs investors, bank financing or a stronger governance framework.

**Treating incorporation as the finish line.** Trade, tax, VAT and sector approvals often follow.

**Using inconsistent information across documents.** Names, addresses, ownership and business activities should match across filings.

**Underestimating foreign-capital steps.** Banking and remittance documentation can affect the overall timeline.

## The Bottom Line

Company registration in Bangladesh is manageable when the sequence is planned properly: choose the structure, prepare consistent documents, complete RJSC incorporation and then move directly into the registrations required for actual operations.
