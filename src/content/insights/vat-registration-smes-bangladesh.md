# VAT Registration for SMEs in Bangladesh: A Practical 2026 Guide

For an SME, the first VAT question is usually not “what is VAT?” but “does it apply to my business now?” The answer depends on turnover, activity and whether the business falls within a category that must register regardless of turnover.

**Important:** VAT thresholds have changed in recent years. The figures below reflect the position described in the supplied 2026 source material. Confirm the current threshold with the NBR VAT Online Portal or a tax adviser before making a filing decision.

## The Turnover Bands

| Annual Turnover | Typical Position |
|---|---|
| Below approx. BDT 30 lakh | Generally outside the formal VAT/Turnover Tax regime, subject to sector rules |
| Approx. BDT 30–50 lakh | Enlistment and Turnover Tax may apply |
| Above approx. BDT 50 lakh | Standard VAT registration is generally mandatory |

Certain activities, including some import, export, manufacturing and specified service categories, may trigger registration regardless of turnover.

## Standard VAT vs Turnover Tax

**Turnover Tax** is simpler and is based on gross turnover, but it generally does not provide the same input tax credit mechanism as standard VAT.

**Standard VAT registration** allows input tax credit where the legal requirements are met. It can also matter commercially because corporate buyers often expect suppliers to issue standard VAT-compliant invoices.

For a B2B SME, the “simpler” regime is not always the best commercial choice. Customer requirements, input costs and growth plans should be considered together.

## The Registration Process

### 1. Confirm the Correct Classification
Determine whether the business is outside the regime, requires enlistment, requires standard VAT registration, or should consider voluntary registration.

### 2. Prepare the Documents
Typical documents may include:

- Trade licence
- TIN certificate
- Incorporation, proprietorship or partnership documents
- Bank details
- Owner/director identification
- Business-premises information
- IRC/ERC where relevant

### 3. Apply Through the VAT Online System
The application is submitted electronically with supporting information and documents.

### 4. Receive the BIN
Once approved, the business receives a Business Identification Number (BIN), which becomes part of its VAT invoicing and return process.

### 5. Update Internal Systems
Registration is not useful without a process behind it. The accounting and invoicing system must be able to track sales, purchases, output VAT, input VAT and required records.

## After Registration

VAT compliance becomes recurring. Businesses generally need to maintain:

- VAT-compliant invoices
- Purchase and sales records
- Supporting challans and documentation
- Regular reconciliations
- Monthly return preparation where required

The exact form, deadline and software requirements should be checked against current NBR guidance.

## Common SME Mistakes

**Using an old threshold.** Recent changes mean an SME that was previously outside the regime may now be in scope.

**Choosing Turnover Tax automatically.** For B2B businesses, the loss of input credit or customer acceptance can matter more than administrative simplicity.

**Failing to reassess as turnover grows.** VAT status can change from one year to the next.

**Registering without fixing invoicing and bookkeeping.** Poor records create problems at return and audit stage.

## The Bottom Line

For SMEs, VAT compliance begins with the correct classification. Confirm the current threshold, choose the appropriate regime for the actual business model, and make sure bookkeeping and invoicing are ready to support the registration from day one.
